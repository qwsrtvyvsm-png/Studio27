Bookings

Do I need to book?

Bookings are recommended, especially on weekends and evenings. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.

How do I make a booking?

Call or text us, or send a request through our contact page. Your booking is confirmed once you hear back from us.

Can I request a specific lady?

Yes. Check the roster to see who’s working, then mention your preference when you book.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give us as much notice as possible. See our Cancellation, Refund & Rescheduling Policy.
